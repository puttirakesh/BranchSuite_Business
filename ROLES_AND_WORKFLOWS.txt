BRANCHSUITE BUSINESS — ROLES, ACCESS, AND WORKFLOWS

HOW ACCESS WORKS
BranchSuite has eight roles. Staff roles use the business workspace; Employees use a separate personal workspace. Access depends on the user's role and assigned company or branch. Managers and scoped staff generally see information only for their assigned locations. Users with multiple roles combine the access granted by those roles. The app's menus show available areas, and the backend also checks permissions when actions are requested.

ROLES

Business Owner
- Has broad business access: workspace settings, companies and branches, user access and roles, employee records, CRM, tasks, reports, and payroll preparation and publication.
- Has additional authority over owner-level access and administrator accounts.

Business Admin
- Manages most business settings, employees, user access, CRM, tasks, and payroll preparation and publication.
- Cannot manage the Owner's account with the same authority as the Owner.

Company Manager
- Oversees operations in assigned companies and can access relevant team information.
- Works with CRM and tasks, reviews team attendance and leave requests, and approves or returns payroll prepared by another user.

Branch Manager
- Has operational access similar to a Company Manager, scoped to assigned branches.
- Works with the branch team, CRM and tasks, reviews attendance and leave requests, and approves or returns payroll prepared by another user.

HR Manager
- Onboards and updates employee records within assigned scope.
- Reviews team attendance and handles leave and attendance-correction workflows and employee requests.

Payroll Officer (PAYROLL_MANAGER)
- Prepares payroll, manages salary inputs, publishes payroll, records manual payment and advance payouts, and views scoped employee and attendance information needed for payroll.
- Cannot mark or correct team attendance or approve payroll.

Sales Executive (SALES_MANAGER)
- Works with scoped leads, deals, tasks, and sales-related reports.
- CRM directory access is limited to assignment information rather than HR employee records.

Employee
- Uses the personal workspace to record their own attendance, request leave or advances, update assigned task status, and view their own published payslips and documents.

COMMON WORKFLOWS

People and access
1. Business Owner/Admin configures workspace structure and user access.
2. HR onboards and updates employee records within its assigned scope.
3. Managers and Payroll access relevant employee information for their work, subject to their company/branch assignments.

Attendance and leave
1. Employees record their own attendance and submit leave or attendance-correction requests.
2. Managers and HR review requests for their assigned teams; HR/Admin handle leave administration.

Payroll
1. Payroll Officer or Business Owner/Admin prepares and calculates a payroll run.
2. A Company Manager or assigned Branch Manager independently reviews it and can approve or return it.
3. The preparer cannot approve their own run. After approval, an authorized preparer can publish it and record payment.

CRM and tasks
1. Managers and Sales work on leads, deals, and team tasks within their company/branch scope.
2. Staff can view and update their own assigned tasks. Creating, assigning, or managing tasks is restricted to task-manager roles.

Employee self-service
Employees use their personal workspace for their own records and requests; they do not receive staff-management access solely by being an employee.

IMPORTANT LIMITS
- Payroll payment and advance payout actions record a manual business action; they do not transfer money through a bank.
- The exact access a user has depends on their assigned company/branch and any additional roles.
- Some general pages, such as dashboard or reports, may be visible to a broader set of staff roles; visibility does not automatically grant permission to perform every action in that area.
