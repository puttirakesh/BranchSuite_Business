BRANCHSUITE BUSINESS - GAP ANALYSIS: CURRENT PROJECT vs REFERENCE APK (v2.3)
Date: 29-10-2026
Companion to: WORKFLOW_GAP_ANALYSIS.txt

WHAT WAS COMPARED
- Reference APK: .apk-analysis/v2.3 (the extracted v2.3 app). It is an
  offline demo prototype: data lives in the browser (localStorage), many
  screens say "demo", and payments are simulated. Its per-role menus are
  recorded in .apk-analysis/v2.3/role-analysis.json.
- Current project: BranchSuite_Business (NestJS backend + Expo frontend).
- The 35 real-world gaps listed in WORKFLOW_GAP_ANALYSIS.txt.

Each item below says what the reference APK does, what the current project
does, and whether it is a gap.

Summary
- Menus and roles: the current project matches the reference. All 8 roles
  have the same bottom bar, sidebar and More sections.
- 8 features exist in the reference APK but are missing or weaker in the
  current project (Section 1). These are the gaps relative to the APK.
- 18 of the 35 real-world gaps are also missing in the reference APK
  (Section 2). Copying the APK will not close them.
- The current project is ahead of the APK in 13 areas (Section 3).


======================================================================
1. IN THE REFERENCE APK BUT MISSING OR WEAKER IN THE CURRENT PROJECT
======================================================================

A1. Sales incentives linked to Won deals                    [MISSING]
    APK: Each Won deal can create one incentive. Payroll Officer or a
    manager approves or rejects it. Pending incentives are excluded from
    payroll; approved ones are included once. Sales staff see "My
    incentive" on their dashboard.
    Current: PayrollIncentive is a manual amount per employee per month.
    It has no link to a deal and no approve/reject status.
    Related real-world gap: #29.

A2. Payroll payment tracking per employee                    [WEAKER]
    APK: Every employee payment has a status (Not initiated, Processing,
    Failed, Confirmed). Failed payments can be retried; a branch batch can
    be processed together.
    Current: PayrollPayment is one manual "paid" record per entry. There is
    no failed state, retry or batch.
    Related real-world gap: #24.

A3. Bank payment file export                                [MISSING]
    APK: Exports a payment file (CSV with employee ID, name, branch, bank
    ending, amount) for the bank upload.
    Current: Payroll exports a review CSV/PDF only. No bank payment file.
    Related real-world gap: #25.

A4. Bank detail change verification                         [MISSING]
    APK: When an employee's bank account changes, it is flagged and must be
    verified ("verify-bank") before pay goes out.
    Current: Bank details can be edited with no verification step.
    Related real-world gap: #22 (maker-checker for bank changes).

A5. Payroll pre-check issues list                            [WEAKER]
    APK: Before calculation, each branch shows a list of checks (missing
    data, exceptions) that must be resolved one by one ("resolve-issue").
    Current: The payroll screen only checks for attendance issues.

A6. Bulk lead assignment                                    [MISSING]
    APK: Managers can select many leads and assign them in one action
    ("bulk-assign").
    Current: Leads are assigned one at a time.
    Related real-world gap: #31 (assignment rules).

A7. Scheduled shift and late marking                         [WEAKER]
    APK: The attendance screen shows the employee's scheduled shift
    (09:00-18:00) and flags late arrivals in team attendance. (The shift is
    fixed demo data, not configurable.)
    Current: No shift times at all. Attendance.lateMinutes exists in the
    database but is never set.
    Related real-world gap: #14.

A8. Subscription plans, limits and platform console          [MISSING]
    APK:
    - Plans enforce limits on users, branches and companies. Adding one
      over the limit is blocked.
    - Checkout with monthly/annual cycle, coupons, invoice payment, cancel
      renewal and plan changes.
    - A platform owner console: plans, coupons, campaigns, demo requests,
      tickets from all businesses, platform metrics.
    Current: Subscription is read-only (planId, cycle, status). No limits
    are enforced anywhere in the backend. No platform owner console; the
    platform-support page is for the business side only.
    Related real-world gap: #34.


======================================================================
2. GAPS THE REFERENCE APK ALSO HAS (copying the APK will not fix these)
======================================================================

Numbers refer to WORKFLOW_GAP_ANALYSIS.txt.

#1  Owner cannot approve payroll. The APK has the same rule: Admin has
    payApprove = false; only Company/Branch Manager approve. This was
    copied from the APK design, so it is a design decision to revisit,
    not a bug.
#2  Statutory deductions (PF, ESI, PT, TDS). The APK only shows a
    placeholder "Demo retirement + tax" deduction.
#3  Leave counts calendar days. The APK has no holiday or weekly-off
    concept at all.
#4  No half-day leave.
#5  Approver can approve a run containing their own salary.
#6  No Finance/Accounts role.
#9  Single-level approvals only.
#11 One-level reporting hierarchy.
#15 No check-in location verification. The APK states it "does not
    collect your location".
#17 No leave accrual, carry-forward or encashment.
#18 No comp-off, maternity/paternity or probation leave rules.
#19 No exit / full-and-final settlement workflow.
#20 No probation, appraisal or increment workflow.
#21 No onboarding checklist or offer letters.
#26 No expense reimbursement.
#28 No statutory payroll register or challans.
#32 No push, email or WhatsApp notifications.
#33 No reminders or escalation for pending approvals.


======================================================================
3. WHERE THE CURRENT PROJECT IS AHEAD OF THE REFERENCE APK
======================================================================

- Real backend and database instead of browser-only demo data.
- Sign-up and password reset with email OTP; login throttling.
- Roles assigned per company, with branch-level access.
- Leave routed to the employee's assigned reporting manager, with
  reassignment of pending leave.
- In-app notification inbox.
- Attendance corrections, including for days with no attendance record.
- Company holiday and weekly-off calendar used in payroll proration.
- Payroll policy (fixed or attendance-based) snapshotted on each run.
- Salary advances recovered automatically through payroll.
- Branch transfers with an effective date that splits salary history.
- Payroll inputs locked once a period is closed.
- Server-side audit log of business actions.
- Real employee document upload and storage.


======================================================================
4. RECOMMENDED ORDER
======================================================================

Step 1 - Close the gaps relative to the APK (features users of v2.3 will
         expect):
  1. A1 Sales incentives from Won deals, with approval.
  2. A3 Bank payment file export, and A2 payment status / retry.
  3. A4 Bank detail change verification.
  4. A7 Shift times and late marking (configurable, not fixed).
  5. A6 Bulk lead assignment.
  6. A5 Payroll pre-check list.
  7. A8 Plan limits enforcement (the platform console can come later).

Step 2 - Fix real-world gaps the APK also has, highest impact first:
  1. #1 and #5 Let the Owner approve payroll; send approvers' own
     salaries to a higher approver.
  2. #3 and #4 Count leave on working days only; allow half days.
  3. #2 Statutory deductions (PF, ESI, PT, TDS).
  4. #19 Exit and full-and-final settlement.
  5. #32 and #33 Push/email notifications and approval reminders.
