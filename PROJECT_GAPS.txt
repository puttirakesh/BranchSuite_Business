BRANCHSUITE BUSINESS - PROJECT GAPS
Last updated: 29-10-2026

Source tags:
  [APK]      The reference APK (v2.3) has this; the current project does not.
  [REAL]     Real businesses need this; neither the APK nor the project has it.
  [VERIFIED] Confirmed by reading the current code.

Priority:  P1 = blocks real use   P2 = expected by customers   P3 = later

Total: 32 gaps (2 in P1, 23 in P2, 7 in P3)


======================================================================
P1 - BLOCKS REAL USE
======================================================================

1.  No statutory payroll deductions                   [REAL]
    No PF, ESI, Professional Tax or TDS; no Form 16 or PF ECR output.

2.  No half-day leave                                 [REAL]
    LeaveRequest.days is a whole number.


======================================================================
P2 - EXPECTED BY CUSTOMERS
======================================================================

Payroll and payments
3.  No reversal or correction after payment           [REAL]
    A confirmed payment cannot be reversed.
4.  No arrears, bonus or off-cycle runs               [REAL]
5.  No expense reimbursement claims                   [REAL]
6.  No limits on salary advances                      [REAL]
7.  No payroll register or PF/ESI challans            [REAL]

Attendance
8.  No check-in location verification                 [REAL]
    No geofence, selfie or device binding.
9.  No automatic absent marking or missed-checkout handling   [REAL]

Leave and HR
10. No leave accrual, carry-forward, encashment or lapse      [REAL]
11. No comp-off, maternity/paternity or probation leave rules [REAL]
12. No exit workflow                                  [REAL]
    No resignation, notice period, clearance, full-and-final settlement
    or relieving letter.
13. No probation, appraisal or increment workflow     [REAL]
14. No onboarding checklist or offer letters          [REAL]
15. Employees cannot request changes to their own records    [REAL]

Roles and approvals
16. No Finance/Accounts role                          [REAL]
    Payroll prepares and also records payment; no second check on money.
17. Role labels do not match role codes               [REAL]
    SALES_MANAGER shown as "Sales Executive"; no real Sales Manager role.
18. Only one business role per person per company in the UI   [REAL]
19. Single-level approvals only                       [REAL]
    No escalation by amount or duration.
20. Delegation only for leave                         [REAL]
    Attendance corrections, advances, incentives and payroll approval
    cannot be reassigned when the approver is away.
21. Only one level of reporting hierarchy             [REAL]
22. Permissions mostly hard-coded by role             [REAL] [VERIFIED]
    No leave/tasks/employees/advances permissions in the permission table,
    so Roles & Permissions cannot customise them.

Notifications
23. In-app notifications only                         [REAL]
    No push, email or WhatsApp for approvals.
24. No reminders or escalation for overdue approvals  [REAL]

Testing
25. Database integration test suite is out of date    [VERIFIED]
    backend/test/app.integration.test.cjs fails 13 of 20 tests. It predates
    company-scoped roles, so test users do not receive their roles and
    later steps fail.


======================================================================
P3 - LATER
======================================================================

Sales and CRM
26. No sales targets or automatic commissions         [REAL]
27. No customer invoicing or payment collection       [REAL]
28. No lead capture integrations or auto-assignment rules     [REAL]
    (website form, WhatsApp, IndiaMART, Meta ads)

Subscription and platform
29. No checkout, coupons, renewal cancel or plan change       [APK] [REAL]
    Needs a payment gateway account.
30. No platform owner console                         [APK]
    APK has plans, coupons, campaigns, demo requests, cross-business
    tickets and platform metrics.

Attendance
31. Late marking does not handle overnight shifts     [VERIFIED]
    A shift such as 22:00-06:00 flags lateness only for check-ins before
    midnight.

Technical
32. Employee documents stored in the database         [REAL]
    Move to object storage with signed URLs.

                       approval delegation.
