BRANCHSUITE BUSINESS - WORKFLOW GAP ANALYSIS
Compared with real-world business roles and practices (Indian SME context)
Date: 29-10-2026

SCOPE
This compares the documented roles and workflows (ROLES_AND_WORKFLOWS.txt) and
the code that enforces them (backend services, access rules, database schema)
with how small and medium Indian businesses run HR, payroll and sales.
Items marked (VERIFIED) were confirmed in the code.


======================================================================
1. CRITICAL - THESE BLOCK A REAL BUSINESS
======================================================================

1. The Business Owner cannot approve payroll (VERIFIED)
   Where: backend/src/modules/payroll/payroll.service.ts, lines 83-88
   Problem: Only a Company Manager or Branch Manager can approve payroll. A
   small business with just an owner and a payroll person can never get
   payroll approved.
   Expected: The owner or a finance head is usually the final approver. The
   approval role should be configurable, with the owner always allowed.

2. No statutory payroll deductions
   Where: payroll schema
   Problem: There is nothing for PF, ESI, Professional Tax or TDS (income
   tax), and no Form 16 or ECR (PF return) output. Salary is only generic
   earnings and deductions.
   Expected: Mandatory for Indian payroll above certain headcounts and
   salaries. Without it, payslips and PF/ESI/TDS filings cannot be relied on.

3. Leave counts every calendar day (VERIFIED)
   Where: backend/src/modules/leave/leave.service.ts, line 287
   Problem: days = end - start + 1, ignoring the weekly offs and holidays the
   company calendar already stores. A Friday-to-Monday leave uses 4 days
   instead of 2.
   Expected: Count working days only, using the company's weekly offs and
   holidays (with configurable "sandwich" rules).

4. No half-day or hourly leave
   Where: LeaveRequest.days is a whole number (Int)
   Expected: Half-day leave is standard everywhere.

5. An approver can approve payroll that includes their own salary (VERIFIED)
   Where: backend/src/modules/payroll/payroll.service.ts, lines 1239-1290
   Problem: The only check is that the approver did not prepare the run. A
   branch manager can approve a run containing their own pay, and the payroll
   officer calculates their own salary.
   Expected: Conflict of interest. Managers' and payroll staff's own salaries
   should go to a higher approver.


======================================================================
2. ROLE AND AUTHORITY GAPS
======================================================================

6. No Finance or Accounts role
   Payment recording, advance payouts and invoices sit with the Payroll
   Officer or Admin. In practice, finance releases money and payroll only
   calculates it. One person both prepares payroll and records payment, so
   funds can move without a second check.

7. Role labels do not match role codes
   SALES_MANAGER is labelled "Sales Executive" and PAYROLL_MANAGER is
   "Payroll Officer". There is no real Sales Manager who oversees a sales
   team's pipeline, targets and reassignments.

8. One business role per person per company
   The Roles & Access screen assigns a single role. Real staff often combine
   roles, for example "Branch Manager + HR" in a small branch. The backend
   can combine roles, but the UI cannot assign them.

9. Approvals have only one level
   Leave, advances and attendance corrections each need one approver. Real
   policies escalate: long leave or a large advance goes to manager, then HR,
   then owner. There are no amount-based or duration-based approval limits.

10. Delegation is incomplete
    Leave can now be reassigned, but attendance corrections, advances and
    payroll approval cannot. If the approver is on leave or has left, those
    requests stay stuck.

11. No reporting hierarchy beyond one level
    There is a single reportingManagerId, with no org chart, skip-level
    visibility or department-head approvals. Departments exist only as text,
    not as an approval structure.

12. The permission table is incomplete (VERIFIED)
    The original permissions migration has no leave.*, tasks.*, employees.*
    or advances.* permissions. These modules enforce access with role names
    written into the code. The "Roles & Permissions" screen therefore cannot
    actually customise permissions, which owners usually expect.

13. Test endpoints are still live (VERIFIED)
    auth/test-dashboard-access, auth/test-crm-create and
    auth/test-business-scope check permissions that do not exist
    (dashboard:read, leads:create). Remove them before release.


======================================================================
3. HR, ATTENDANCE AND LEAVE GAPS
======================================================================

14. No shifts or late tracking (VERIFIED)
    Attendance.lateMinutes exists, but nothing ever sets it. There are no
    shift times, grace period, overtime, night shifts or rotating rosters.
    Retail and branch businesses depend on these.

15. No check-in verification
    There is no geofence, branch location check, selfie or device binding, so
    an employee can check in from anywhere. This is the most common
    attendance fraud.

16. No automatic absent marking or missed-checkout handling
    Missing days block payroll (BLOCK_CALCULATION is the only option), and
    nothing auto-closes an open shift.

17. No leave accrual, carry-forward, encashment or lapse
    Balances are allocated by hand each year. Real policies accrue monthly
    (for example 1.5 days a month), carry a capped balance into the next
    year, and pay out unused leave on exit.

18. Thin leave types
    Missing: comp-off for working on holidays, maternity/paternity leave
    rules, probation restrictions, and blocking leave on mandatory days.

19. No exit (offboarding) workflow
    Status EXITED and an exit date exist, but there is no resignation
    request, notice period, clearance checklist (asset return, access
    revocation), full-and-final settlement or relieving/experience letters.

20. No probation or confirmation stage, and no appraisal or increment
    workflow. Salary revisions exist but are not tied to reviews.

21. No onboarding paperwork
    Documents can be uploaded, but there is no checklist (KYC, offer letter
    acceptance, bank verification), no document expiry tracking, and no
    generated offer letters.

22. Employees cannot request changes to their own records
    They cannot ask to update bank details, address or emergency contact
    with HR approval. Bank-detail changes in particular need maker-checker
    approval.


======================================================================
4. PAYROLL GAPS
======================================================================

23. No arrears, off-cycle or bonus runs. There is one run per branch per
    month, and incentives are a single manual figure.

24. No reversal after payment. The statuses are DRAFT, CALCULATED, APPROVED
    and PAID, with no correction or reversal path once paid.

25. No bank payout file. Payment is recorded by hand. Real businesses export
    an NEFT/bulk-transfer file in their bank's format.

26. No expense reimbursement. There is no claim, bill-upload and
    approve-then-payroll flow. Travel and fuel reimbursement is a basic
    expectation.

27. Advances have no limits. There is no cap (for example one month's net
    pay), no eligibility rule, and no check against outstanding advances.

28. No payroll register or compliance reports, such as a salary register,
    PF/ESI challans or bank reconciliation.


======================================================================
5. CRM AND SALES GAPS
======================================================================

29. No sales targets or commissions. Incentives are entered by hand instead
    of being calculated from won deals.

30. No customer invoicing or payment collection. Quotes do not become
    invoices or orders. BillingInvoice covers the app's own subscription
    billing.

31. No lead capture integrations (website form, WhatsApp, IndiaMART, Meta
    ads) and no automatic lead assignment rules.


======================================================================
6. PLATFORM GAPS
======================================================================

32. Notifications are in-app only. There are no push, email or WhatsApp
    alerts for approvals, so managers miss pending requests. The code only
    sends email for OTPs.

33. No reminders or overdue escalation for pending approvals, task deadlines
    or payroll cut-off.

34. Subscription is read-only. There is no online payment or renewal, which
    the README already notes.

35. Employee documents are stored inside the database (Bytes column). This
    does not scale. Use object storage with signed URLs.


======================================================================
7. WHAT ALREADY MATCHES REAL PRACTICE
======================================================================

- The person who prepares payroll cannot approve it.
- Payroll inputs are locked once a period closes.
- Each payroll run keeps a snapshot of the pay policy it used.
- Data is separated by company and branch.
- Leave is routed to the employee's reporting manager.
- Actions are audit-logged.
- Login attempts are throttled.
- Advances are recovered through payroll.
- Transfers between branches take an effective date.


======================================================================
8. SUGGESTED ORDER TO FIX
======================================================================

1. Items 1 and 5 (who can approve payroll). Quick, and unblocks small
   businesses.
2. Items 3, 4 and 13 (leave day counting, half-day leave, removing test
   endpoints). Small code changes.
3. Item 2 (statutory deductions). The largest piece, but Indian customers
   cannot use payroll for real without it.
4. Items 14-16 (shifts and attendance verification) and item 19 (exit
   workflow).
5. The rest by customer demand.
